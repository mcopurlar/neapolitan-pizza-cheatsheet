Key Tips:

  • Bring the poolish to room temp for 30 mins before making the main dough.
  • Add the olive oil only after the flour is mixed in to build gluten properly.
  • When shaping balls, fold the dough underneath itself to trap the air like a balloon.

Baking:

  • Use semolina flour to stretch your dough for a soft and crunchy texture.
  • Preheat your pizza stone in the oven on max temperature for at least 1 hour.
