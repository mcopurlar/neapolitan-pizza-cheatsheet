A W300 flour is a strong flour built for 48-hour fermentation. Ball weight 240–260g gives a 28–33cm pizza. Total dough ~1.73kg.

Total Timeline ≈ 48 hours

  • 12–17 min · Knead in mixer (water in two parts)
  • 30 min–2 hrs · Bulk rest, room temp
  • ~20 hrs · Bulk in fridge at 4–6°C (16–30 OK)
  • 30 min–2 hrs · Balled, room temp
  • 18–24 hrs · Balled, in fridge at 4–6°C
  • 1–2 hrs · Balls back at room temp
  • 90–110 sec · Bake at ~450°C

Fermentation is split in half: about 24 hrs in bulk and 24 hrs in balls, both in the fridge. The room-temp rests are short and depend on your kitchen temperature and dough size.

Kneading Tips:

  • Add the water in two parts. The first 60% builds the gluten network that can then absorb the last 10%, which goes in slowly after the salt.
  • Aim for a final dough temperature of 23–25°C. Use colder water if your kitchen or mixer runs warm.
  • Done when the dough is elastic, does not tear easily, and comes together as a smooth ball.
  • No mixer? Dissolve the yeast in the water, add most of the flour, then the salt. Knead 8–10 minutes until no longer sticky, rest 30 minutes, then fold to build strength. Repeat rest + fold if it still feels weak.
  • Fold the dough a few times inside the oiled container before the bulk rest to make it homogeneous.

Fermentation, Balling & Baking:

  • Cold fermentation is flexible: bulk anywhere from 16 to 30 hours, balls 18 to 24 hours. Good for planning around your schedule.
  • The room-temp rests before each fridge stage are shorter in a warm kitchen or with a small batch, longer when cold.
  • Don't oversize the balls for a given pizza diameter. A heavier ball on the same 30cm pizza gives a denser, less light result.
  • Stretch in semolina so no damp patches stick. Press from the centre to push the gas into the rim, then finish on your knuckles and let gravity do the work.
  • Leopard spotting on the crust means the fermentation and the oven temperature were right. The centre should be thin, the crumb open and light.

Example schedule (pizza Saturday 7pm):

Thursday 6:00pm

Knead, fold into the oiled container, rest on the counter.

Thursday 7:00pm

Bulk container into the fridge.

Friday 5:00pm

Ball into 7 × 250g, rest on the counter.

Friday 6:00pm

Ball container into the fridge.

Saturday 5:30pm

Balls out of the fridge to warm up.

Saturday 7:00pm

Stretch, top, bake 90–110 seconds.

Both fridge stages have slack in them, so shifting any step by a few hours is fine. Room-temp rests assume ~20–24°C; in a warmer kitchen keep them at the short end.
