Swapping yeast types:

By weight, fresh : active dry : instant ≈ 3 : 1.25 : 1. Fresh yeast is mostly water and active dry has fewer live cells per gram, so 3g fresh ≈ 1.25g active dry ≈ 1g instant. Instant goes straight into the flour, active dry should be dissolved in warm water first, fresh gets crumbled into the water.

Reading the timelines:

Room-temperature timings assume roughly 20°C unless a sheet says otherwise. Yeast works about 1.5–1.7× faster at 26°C, so in a warm kitchen shorten the room-temp stages or reduce the yeast, and always go by how the dough looks rather than the clock.
